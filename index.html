Approach

How we work.

Four lenses that shape every screen we ship — what we stand for, the process we follow, who we serve, and how we make sure no one's left behind.

01

What we stand for. Principles.

The pillars of our institutional design experience.

Trust & security
Every screen reinforces customer confidence at the moments money or data is on the line.
Accessibility first
WCAG AA is the floor, not the ceiling. Focus, contrast, motion — all covered, all measured.
Scalability
Built for the lifetime of the platform. Patterns work for one team or one hundred.
Clarity
Pages are skimmable. Hierarchy, copy, and grouping always serve the customer's next decision.
02

How we work. Process.

From customer signals to delivery and measurement.

01 — Discovery
Signals & opportunities
User research and stakeholder context shape the brief and pillar priorities.
02 — Definition
Problem & principles
Define audience needs, success metrics, and design principles for the work.
03 — Ideation
Concepts & flows
Sketches, system mapping and concept validation against component spec.
04 — Validation
Test & iterate
Prototypes go through usability, accessibility, and brand QA — looped back.
05 — Delivery
Hand-off & measure
Specs ship with adoption metrics so improvements stay observable in production.
UX Review principle
No design ships alone. Every flow goes through a peer UX review — two designers sign off against the principles checklist before hand-off. We catch the things one set of eyes always misses.
2 reviewers · Principles checklist · Before every hand-off
03

Built for everyone. Audience.

Different audiences pull different value out of the system.

For designers
Spend time on judgment calls, not on rebuilding inputs. The library is the floor; everything you do builds on it.
For engineers
Pre-tested components with accessibility and theming baked in. Works first time. Less reinventing, fewer regressions.
For the business
Faster delivery, less rework, lower per-screen cost. The portfolio compounds — improvements in one place lift the whole.
04

Care for everyone. Inclusion.

A system that works for the full range of Apex customers.

Focus support
Reasoning, decision orderliness, and customer disclosure paths so confused customers can always re-orient.
Elderly-friendly design
Larger text, high contrast, simple navigation — usable for customers across the age range.
Fatigue & late-shift care
Lower-contrast variants, calmer states, reduced-motion options for analysts who work long hours.
